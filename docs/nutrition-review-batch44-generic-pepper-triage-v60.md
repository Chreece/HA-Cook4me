# Nutrition review Batch 44 — generic pepper triage (v60)

Batch 44 is intentionally small. It creates **no nutrition binding** and changes only one ambiguity class: labels that say generic `Pepper` without identifying the pepper species/type.

## Frozen baseline

- source evidence SHA-256: `e3b22f9b0834a79ffcc471d7bc61c07f08ce1f63da312c2ac36e84633c2931ac`
- recorded review targets after Batch 43: **5,080**
- unresolved targets: **1,239**
- manual-family lane: **321**
- context-heavy lane: **918**
- active explicit evidence requirements: **19**

## Exact small scope

Only these 11 exact targets move from manual-family review to context-heavy review:

- the three provider identities named exactly `Pepper`;
- `*Pepper`;
- `Pepper (a little)` / `Pepper, a little`;
- `Pepper (for finishing)` and its `a little` variant;
- `Ground pepper`;
- the two `Coarsely ground pepper` presentation variants.

Together they account for **2,478 recorded ingredient usages**.

The reason is identity ambiguity, not preparation: plain `pepper` does not establish black pepper, white pepper, chili pepper, Sichuan pepper, pink peppercorn, or another pepper identity. Candidate search rank is not identity proof.

## Controls deliberately left manual

Named identities remain outside this rule, including `Ground Sichuan pepper`, `Sichuan pepper powder`, `Sichuan peppercorns`, `Pink peppercorn`, `Pink peppercorns`, `Creole pepper`, `Sansho pepper`, and `Çarliston pepper`.
