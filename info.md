**HA-Cook4me** connects compatible KRUPS/Tefal Cook4Me and Cookeo Wi-Fi cookers with a recipe, pantry and meal-planning workspace in Home Assistant.

### Main features

- **Official recipes:** Browse illustrated recipes, ingredients, servings and guided cooking steps.
- **Daily and weekly meals:** Find suitable meals from pantry stock and personal diet preferences; plan by day and meal type.
- **My kitchen:** Manage packages, stock quantities, storage places, expiring products, costs and nutrition.
- **Product and receipt capture:** Scan or manually review barcodes, dates, labels and optional AI-assisted receipt details.
- **Connected cooker:** Inspect cooking-state entities and send supported official recipes; optional notifications and spoken announcements.
