**HA-Cook4me** connects compatible KRUPS/Tefal Cook4Me and Cookeo Wi-Fi cookers to Home Assistant and adds an integrated Recipe Hub.

### Highlights

- **Official recipes:** Browse recipe details, ingredients, photos and cooking steps.
- **Pantry-aware suggestions:** Find recipes based on what you already have.
- **Diet and allergy filters:** Keep recommendations aligned with your preferences.
- **Send to cooker:** Send supported official recipes directly to the connected appliance.
- **Home Assistant integration:** Live device status, notifications, shopping lists and smart-home workflows.
